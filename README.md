make.fun bounty entry: one original line roasting a smart fridge, at most
200 characters. The line is the whole entry.

## The line

> At 2 a.m. my smart fridge made me sign in before I could reach my own leftovers — my midnight snack now needs a password, a bouncer, and a firmware update.

155 characters. A scene, not a quip: a 2 a.m. timestamp, a concrete
grievance (signing in to reach your own leftovers), and the punchline as a
three-item escalation — password, bouncer, firmware update. It dodges the
crowded cracks (firmware terms-and-conditions, the camera watching you,
the slow ice maker) and roasts the fridge where it lives: its app, its
sign-in, its updates.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py  # writes entry.txt to $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR (fallback /output)
```

`main.py` (stdlib only) validates the line is 1–200 characters on a single
line, writes it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (fallback `/output`),
then reads it back to verify the written bytes match.
